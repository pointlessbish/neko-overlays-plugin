Info bar styles

All styles with made-up numbers. This page is served by the plugin on your own computer and loads nothing from the internet itself. Google fonts are downloaded once by the plugin and kept on your computer, so a style may show a fallback font for a few seconds the first time.

Chat styles

The Neko Chat source in the same styles, with made-up messages. The StreamElements message is hidden by the default Hidden users list, and now and then a message is deleted, which removes it from the overlay. Emote and badge pictures are loaded from their own servers, so they need an internet connection.
